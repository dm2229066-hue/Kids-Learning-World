Privacy Policy — Kids Learning World

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Designed for Children Aged 2–10

Summary: Kids Learning World does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information from children or parents. All learning progress, reward stars, and drawings stay locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Kids Learning World is designed with strict child privacy protection in mind (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy). We do not require user accounts or login, and we do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or home addresses
  • Photos, videos, microphone audio recordings, or contacts
  • Precise GPS location or behavioral user profiles

2. Local Storage & Offline Support

Reward stars, unlocked animal badges, language selection (English/Hindi), age-group settings, and saved drawings from the Coloring Studio are stored exclusively on your device using local browser/app storage. Kids Learning World works offline after initial installation.

3. Voice & Sound Features

Voice pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine (Web Speech API) and locally synthesized sound effects. No voice audio is recorded or transmitted to external servers.

4. Child-Safe Advertising (Google AdMob)

In Android builds using Google AdMob, ads are strictly configured for child-directed treatment:

  • Non-Personalized Ads Only: Personalized ad tracking is disabled (npa: true).
  • Child-Directed Flags: Requests are tagged with tagForChildDirectedTreatment: true, tagForUnderAgeOfConsent: true, and MaxAdContentRating: General.
  • Parent Control: Parents can turn Banner Ads on or off anytime inside the math-protected Parent Zone.

5. Parental Gate & Child Safety

Kids Learning World contains no chat rooms, no social media integrations, and no outbound web links accessible to children. Settings, ad controls, and progress reset options are protected behind a Parent Verification math challenge.

6. Deleting Your Data

Because all progress is stored locally on your device, parents can erase all saved stars, badges, and drawings anytime by tapping Reset Local Learning Progress inside the Parent Zone, or by uninstalling the app / clearing browser site data.
